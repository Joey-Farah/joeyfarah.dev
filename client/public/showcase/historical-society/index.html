Walk 170 years in 40 minutes

A free walking tour of Halvorsen Falls, right on your phone. No app to download.

Inside the schoolhouse

The tour ends at our museum, in the town's first brick school. Three rooms, free to visit.

The mill that fed the valley

Ledgers, grain scales and a working model of the mill wheel.

Ice harvest

Saws and tongs from the winters when the river was cut into blocks and shipped by rail.

The 1900 classroom

Slates, inkwells and the teacher's bell. Kids are welcome to ring it.
